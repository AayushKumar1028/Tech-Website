05 / Evaluation

Rated twice: the prototype, then the final

A design is a hypothesis until it has fallen off something.

Both builds were dropped off the same balcony and both eggs came back cracked — but not for the same reason.

Both verdicts are below, with the video behind each one.

05A / evaluation of prototype

Evaluation of prototype

Build 01, judged honestly.

It failed — and these are the four things we took out of it before starting again.

what happened — drop recording

prototype drop · 00:14assets/media/prototype-drop.mp4

The prototype drop, filmed from the landing side.

Watch the canopy on the way down — it stays shut, so the shell reaches the ground at close to full speed.

Evaluation of the prototype: what worked, what did not work, what we could improve and what we learned
Review point Findings
What worked
  • The shell folded shut around the egg and stayed folded — no re-taping between drops.
  • Four tape lines were enough to keep the box hanging roughly level.
What didn't work
  • The canopy never stayed open, so the box arrived at close to full speed.
  • The open box had almost no padding to speak of; the egg moved inside it and cracked.
  • Nothing was sealed, so tape gave way at the corners on impact.
What we can improve
  • Stop depending on the canopy opening: whatever happens up there, the shell has to survive full speed.
  • Pad the seat so the egg cannot move at all, and double the shell walls.
  • Seal the package so it stays in one piece at the moment of contact.
What we learned
  • A part that only sometimes works is not a part, and we could not repeat a drop, so we could not trust one.
  • The damage started at the corners and seams — the flat panels were the strongest parts of the box.
Verdict

idea failed Prototype retired, then rebuilt from scratch around a sealed shell.

the result — the egg after landing

three angles, minutes after impact

One egg, three angles, minutes after landing.

The damage sits in a single patch where the shell met the ground, and the plates of shell around it are loose rather than crushed.

Nothing held the egg still, so it slid inside the open box and took the whole hit itself — which is the failure the review table above is about.

05B / evaluation of final

Evaluation of final

Build 02, judged the same way: same balcony, same egg, same checklist.

The package did nearly everything it was designed to do — and the egg still did not survive.

what happened — drop recording

final drop · 00:40assets/media/final-drop.mp4

The final drop, filmed from the top of the stairwell.

The canopy keeps the fall slow enough to follow all the way down, the shell lands in one piece — and the egg is cracked when it comes out.

Evaluation of the final build: what worked, what did not work, what we could improve and what we learned
Review point Findings
What worked
  • The sealed shell landed in one piece: every taped seam held and the bundle stayed a single object.
  • The canopy kept the fall slow — slow enough that the camera could follow the rig the whole way down.
  • Nothing tore loose on impact — no tube burst open, no panel came free.
What didn't work
  • The shell crumpled exactly where it landed — the tube ends dented inward, that crush reached the egg, and the egg cracked.
  • Keeping the egg still was not enough on its own — it stayed put and broke anyway.
  • All that cardstock and tape adds mass, and mass is what we are fighting.
What we can improve
  • Give the egg a crumple zone of its own — something between the egg and the shell that collapses instead of the egg.
  • Reinforce the ends of the tubes, because that is exactly where the bundle folds.
  • Trim the canopy and the lines so the packed rig carries less momentum into the floor.
What we learned
  • A sealed shell keeps the package together but passes the impact straight through to the egg.
  • The failure moved instead of disappearing: build 01 lost the egg because it slid, build 02 held it still and it cracked anyway.
Verdict

idea failed It held together and still lost the egg — the package is sound, the cushioning is not.
