05 / Evaluation

Testing and results

A design is a hypothesis until it has fallen off something. Here is how we tested, what the drops showed, and what we would change next time.

> test method

How we tested

  1. 1 Dropped the device from the same height each time.
  2. 2 Checked the egg for cracks and leaks after every drop.
  3. 3 Recorded what happened and what to change next.

> audit

Did we meet the criteria?

Every criterion from the Problem stage, checked against the design feature that answers it.

Criteria scorecard with the design feature that satisfies each one
Criterion Met? Evidence / design feature
The egg survives with no cracks or leaks confirm at final drop Padding and the parachute reduce the impact on the shell.
The device stays in one piece after landing confirm at final drop Taped seams and a sturdy cardstock shell.
Only the provided materials were used yes Everything came from the supplied kit.
Built within the time and size limits yes A simple four-step build kept us on schedule.

> findings

What worked well

THE PARACHUTE

Slowed the fall and helped the shell land upright.

THE PADDING

Held the egg still and absorbed shock from the landing.

TESTING EARLY

Trial drops let us find and fix problems before the final test.

main lesson

A longer stop means a smaller force on the egg.

> next iteration

What could be better

Every honest evaluation ends with a list of things that were still wrong.

Problems with the design and the changes we would make
Problem What we would change
The parachute can drift or tangle. Use light, evenly spaced lines and pack the canopy carefully.
Extra padding adds weight. Test different padding thicknesses to find the best balance.
We only tested a few drops. Run more trials from different heights for more reliable results.

> conclusion

What we learned

  • ▸ Engineers protect fragile things by making impacts last longer and spreading out the force.
  • ▸ The SPICE process helped us plan, build, test and improve step by step.
  • ▸ Testing and fixing problems made our design stronger.
