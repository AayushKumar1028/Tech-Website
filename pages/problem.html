> the real constraint

The criteria fight each other

Keep 25% of the egg visible, and you have to leave a hole in the armour. Make the package smaller than the egg, and you lose the volume that padding needs to work. Spend tape on the parachute, and there is less for the seams. Every decision removes an option somewhere else.
