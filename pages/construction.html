> fabrication

Step-by-step build

Four steps, in order. Skipping one is how you end up with a rattling egg.

  1. 01

    Build the shell

    Cut and fold cardstock into a box with room around the egg.

  2. 02

    Add padding

    Line every wall and the floor so the egg cannot rattle inside the shell.

  3. 03

    Make the parachute

    Cut the canopy, add four equal strings, and tie them to the shell.

  4. 04

    Seal and check

    Place the egg, close the lid, tape the seams, and untangle the lines.

> debug log

Problems and fixes

Three things went wrong during the build. All three were found by handling the package before the drop, not by hoping.

Problems found during construction and how each was fixed
Problem Fix
FAULT 01

The egg rattled inside the shell.

Added more padding on every side until the egg sat snugly.
FAULT 02

The parachute strings tangled.

Cut the strings to equal lengths and folded the canopy neatly.
FAULT 03

Tape peeled off the seams.

Added extra tape and glue at the corners.
