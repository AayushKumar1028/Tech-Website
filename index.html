> governing equation

F = Δp / Δt

Force equals the change in momentum divided by the time it takes to stop.

The longer the egg takes to stop, the smaller the force on its shell.

Run the numbers

slow the fall

Air resistance and a parachute cut the speed that the egg arrives with.

stretch the stop

Padding and crumple zones buy the milliseconds that make the impact survivable.

spread the force

A bigger contact area means less pressure on any single spot of the shell.

contain the payload

If the egg leaves the container, nothing else on this list matters.

> selected build

We went with Kunj's sketch

Four sketches, five concepts, one winner.

Kunj's design stacked two ideas that protect the egg in different ways: a parachute that lowers the landing speed, and a cardstock case that takes over if the landing is still rough.

  • 1 Parachute canopySlows the fall before impact.
  • 2 Suspension tape linesHold the shell hanging level underneath.
  • 3 Outer cardstock shellTakes the first hit and crumples instead of the egg.
  • 4 Egg seatHolds the egg still and absorbs the shock.
